The anchor · provided free

University School of Medicine

A real campus at the town's heart — medical school, library, anatomy and clinical halls around a central quadrangle — the reason the town exists.
#3–15 · Wellness Way

Care within reach

Every tier needs care: students get low-cost urgent visits at Thacher Student Health, families get pediatrics at Brookfield Family Physicians, seniors get home visits from Innisfree — thirteen facilities on the Wellness Way spine, minutes from every district.

#14–15 · the quiet east

Ageing in place

Halcyon House senior living and Stillpoint hospice in the leafy east — a full care loop from independent living to end-of-life.
Layout & placement

Why things sit where they sit

Streets first, then zoning: the medical campus anchors the centre, hospital + EMS share the Wellness Way spine a block apart, seniors rest in the quiet east, shopping runs along Commerce Blvd, and the K-12 sits inside family neighbourhoods — every address within two blocks of a through-street for emergency access.
Decision-making

The trade-offs

Two donated anchors freed ~$4.5M, buying coverage over scale — thirteen neighbourhood facilities instead of one mega-campus. We chose the mall over a second park, senior care in the quiet east over downtown frontage, and kept $50k unspent rather than force a 27th building.
